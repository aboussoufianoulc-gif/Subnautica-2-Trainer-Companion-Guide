## 🌊 Overview

The ocean of Subnautica 2 is vast, gorgeous, and — let us be honest — occasionally unforgiving. This repository exists for the diver who wants to steer their own journey rather than be steered by it. Think of it as a tide chart, a compass, and a logbook rolled into one companion suite, purpose-built for enthusiasts who want a tailored experience.

Where most repositories hand you a wall of scripts and wish you luck, this one hands you a narrative. Every section is written as if a fellow diver is sitting beside you, explaining what each knob does, why it matters, and how to keep your journey smooth across the Windows ecosystem.

The core idea is simple: **you should be able to shape your play session, not fight it.** Whether you want to slow down the tension, magnify the sense of exploration, or just tweak the visual rhythm to match your monitor and mood, this companion suite is the calm current that lets you drift rather than paddle.

---

## 🐚 The Philosophy Behind This Project

Every great underwater story begins with a single breath. Our approach mirrors that: start small, adapt quickly, expand only when it feels natural.

We believe that altering your own single-player adventure should feel like adjusting the lighting in your living room, not rewiring the whole house. The companion suite therefore focuses on:

- **Clarity over complexity** — each option is described in plain language, with real-world diving metaphors to anchor the concept.
- **Reversibility** — every adjustment is a toggle, never a trap. Return to your original state as easily as surfacing for air.
- **Local-first design** — your settings live with you, on your machine, configured to respect your privacy and your preferences.
- **Longevity** — the suite is designed with 2026 in mind, but structured so it remains legible in 2027 and beyond.

## 🚀 Getting Started

Getting situated is a matter of a few deliberate steps, like gearing up before a dive.

1. **Prepare your environment.** Ensure your Windows installation is up to date, and confirm your game version is the one you intend to accompany.
2. **Acquire the companion package.** Use the macro provided earlier in this document to obtain the current build.
3. **Unpack into a dedicated folder.** Keeping everything in one place makes it trivial to relocate or remove later.
4. **Launch the companion dashboard.** It will greet you with a short orientation walkthrough that explains each panel.
5. **Adjust one setting at a time.** Dive, tweak, surface, reflect. Repeat as desired.
6. **Save your profile.** Store multiple profiles for different moods, difficulties, or playthroughs.

That's it. No sprawling rituals, no obscure incantations — just a calm descent into customization.

## 🧭 Configuration Layers Explained

The suite organizes configuration into three concentric layers, like the zones of the ocean itself:

- **Sunlight Layer (Basics)** — presets and simple toggles. Perfect for a first dive.
- **Twilight Layer (Advanced)** — fine-grained tuning of individual modules, including timing, display, and audio interactions.
- **Abyss Layer (Expert)** — granular control for enthusiasts who want to script behaviors or craft bespoke configurations.

Each layer is open to everyone; nothing is gated behind artificial walls. The naming is merely a navigational aid.

## ⚡ Performance Tuning Notes

To keep your journey smooth:

- Prefer **windowed borderless** mode if you often swap between monitors.
- Avoid stacking multiple overlays at once; the suite is designed to work cleanly on its own.
- Keep your GPU drivers current to avoid visual hiccups during transitions.
- Monitor memory usage if you run the game for multiple hours; the companion logs will tell you if anything drifts.

A well-tuned dive is a quiet dive.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Is this a replacement for the game?**
No. It is a companion suite that sits beside your installation, never on top of it.

**Will it interfere with my progress?**
Adjustments are reversible, and your save data is treated with care. Nothing is silently rewritten.

**Does it work on Windows 10 and 11?**
Yes, both are first-class citizens.

**Can I use it on multiple machines?**
Yes — keep your profile folder in sync and your settings travel with you.

**Is there a way to reset everything?**
Yes. A single "return to shore" option restores original defaults.

**Do I need to be online?**
Once configured, the suite works entirely offline.
